# Иллюстрации карточек v3

Встроенный ImageGen, новые изображения (не редактирование). WebP в `public/assets/`. Все названия материалов остаются живым HTML. Три тематических фотографии повторяются по смысловым группам программ; единая фотографическая основа обложки используется для всех 14 методичек с индивидуальными названиями.

## class-game-v3.webp

Use case: product-mockup. Asset: photographic illustration for autumn Russian education catalogue, no text. Photorealistic close-up of a small dark walnut chessboard with three sculptural brushed brass chess pieces and a single cream paper card, on rich chocolate wood table with rust linen. Beautiful lateral autumn sun, cinematic but clear natural material detail, editorial still life, horizontal 3:2 crop. Warm cream, copper, terracotta and espresso only, no lime, no people, no lettering, no logos, no watermark. Hero objects fill central 70%, readable even at 300px wide.

## class-plan-v3.webp

Use case: product-mockup. Asset: photographic illustration for autumn expert education course cards, no text. Photorealistic overhead editorial still life of several ivory planning sheets with simple geometric printed route lines but absolutely no letters or numbers, warm brass compass and fine pencil on russet linen and dark chocolate desktop. One curled page, directional warm window light, tactile paper grain and natural soft shadows. Sophisticated real photography, horizontal 3:2, main objects central, restrained ivory/copper/rust palette. No people, no screens, no brands, no readable text, no lime.

No people, no text, no logos, no green, no watermark.

## guide-cover-v3.webp

Use case: product-mockup. Asset: realistic blank digital-guide illustrative cover used under live HTML lettering in a web carousel. Single closed tall portrait-format linen-bound booklet viewed exactly front-on, no perspective skew, centered filling 80% height and 65% width of horizontal 3:2 image. Plain warm ivory textured front cover completely blank, subtle narrow copper cloth spine on left, soft shadows under object, brown walnut table and rust linen softly blurred behind it. Refined autumn editorial product photo. Clear rectangle with unobstructed central area for title overlay; no text, no symbols, no logos, no people. The whole book is visible with breathing room.
